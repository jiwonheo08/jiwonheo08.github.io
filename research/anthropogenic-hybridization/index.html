B.S. project · Spined loaches

Anthropogenic Hybridization in Spined Loaches

How human alteration of freshwater connectivity can create secondary contact between historically isolated lineages.

Question

What happens when people reconnect isolated habitats?

Human-mediated changes to river connectivity can create new opportunities for contact between lineages that evolved separately.

Focus

Hybridization after secondary contact

This project examines genetic mixing following anthropogenic habitat connection and asks how quickly previously isolated populations can become homogenized.

Perspective

Evolution in human-altered landscapes

The study connects population genetics with a broader question: how rapidly can human landscape change redirect evolutionary trajectories?

Affiliation

Department of Biology, University of Rochester. I conduct my doctoral research in the Uy Lab.
