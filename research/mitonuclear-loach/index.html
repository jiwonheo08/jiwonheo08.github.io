M.S. project · Spined loaches

Mitonuclear Tree Discordance in Korean and Japanese Spined Loaches

Using discordant mitochondrial and nuclear histories to reconstruct introgression and biogeographic history.

Question

When evolutionary trees disagree

Mitochondrial and nuclear genomes can preserve different histories. This project asks what processes generated that discordance among Korean and Japanese spined loaches.

Approach

Historical introgression and phylogenetic comparison

The project compares genomic and phylogenetic patterns to distinguish alternative explanations for discordance and to evaluate historical gene flow among lineages.

Context

East Asian freshwater biogeography

The work uses spined loaches as a system for understanding how historical connectivity and isolation shaped present-day evolutionary relationships.

Affiliation

Department of Biology, University of Rochester. I conduct my doctoral research in the Uy Lab.
