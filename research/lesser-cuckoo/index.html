M.S. project · Lesser Cuckoo

Polymorphism in the Lesser Cuckoo

An evolutionary study of phenotypic and genomic variation in an avian brood parasite.

Question

Why does polymorphism persist?

This project examines variation in the Lesser Cuckoo and asks how distinct forms arise and remain in a population.

Approach

Linking phenotype and genomic variation

The work connects observable polymorphism with genomic data to investigate candidate evolutionary mechanisms underlying the variation.

System

Avian brood parasitism

The Lesser Cuckoo provides a complementary bird system for studying how diversity is generated and maintained within species.

Affiliation

Department of Biology, University of Rochester. I conduct my doctoral research in the Uy Lab.
