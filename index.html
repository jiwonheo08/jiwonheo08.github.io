The Origin and Maintenance of Island Bird Species

Selection, Introgression, and Hybrid Incompatibilities in Myzomela Honeyeaters

My doctoral research uses island honeyeaters to ask how species originate, exchange genes, and remain distinct. The project brings together four connected questions: selection, introgression, hybrid incompatibilities, and extra-pair paternity.
